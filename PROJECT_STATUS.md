## Long-Term Goal

ProofSetu Protocol aims to become an open-source verifiable workflow proof layer for documents, milestones, invoices, grant deliverables, donation usage, cross-border service work, and AI-assisted decisions.

Private documents stay off-chain. Only hashes, timestamps, proof metadata, and verification references are used for transparent verification.
