# Use Cases

ProofSetu Protocol is designed for real-world workflows where proof, trust, and verification matter.

The goal is to create tamper-evident proof records without exposing private documents or sensitive data.

---

## 1. Freelancer Milestone Proof

A freelancer can create a proof record when a project milestone is completed.

Example:

* Landing page completed
* Client dashboard delivered
* Design files submitted
* Demo video shared
* Final handover completed

This helps reduce disputes between freelancers and clients by creating a timestamped proof record.

---

## 2. Invoice Approval Proof

A service provider can create a proof record when an invoice is submitted, approved, or acknowledged.

Example:

* Invoice submitted to client
* Invoice approved by manager
* Payment pending confirmation
* Payment completed reference added later

This can support future payment, stablecoin, or milestone settlement workflows on Stellar.

---

## 3. NGO Donation Usage Proof

An NGO can create proof records for donation usage events.

Example:

* Food distribution completed
* Medical camp expense recorded
* Education material distributed
* Community service activity completed

The public can verify that an event was recorded without exposing private receipts, beneficiary details, or internal documents.

---

## 4. Startup Grant Deliverable Proof

A startup or open-source team can create proof records for grant deliverables and milestone submissions.

Example:

* MVP completed
* Testnet deployed
* Documentation submitted
* Demo video published
* Community feedback collected

This can help grant reviewers verify deliverable timelines and public progress.

---

## 5. Hospital Document Submission Proof

A hospital administration team can create proof records when important insurance or operational documents are submitted.

Example:

* Claim document submitted
* Query response uploaded
* Final bill prepared
* Discharge summary completed
* Approval document received

Private patient documents are not stored on-chain. Only proof hashes and metadata are used for verification.

---

## 6. AI-Assisted Decision Proof

An AI workflow can create a proof record when it generates or supports an important decision.

Example:

* AI-generated document summary
* AI-assisted claim checklist
* AI-generated invoice review
* AI-supported approval recommendation
* AI workflow audit event

This creates an accountability trail for AI-assisted workflows.

---

## 7. Cross-Border Service Proof

A business or freelancer working with international clients can create proof records for service delivery events.

Example:

* Work delivered to overseas client
* Client approval recorded
* Invoice submitted
* Payment milestone completed
* Handover document shared

This aligns with Stellar’s strengths in cross-border payments and low-cost financial infrastructure.

---

## Summary

ProofSetu Protocol can support many trust-based workflows:

* Work proof
* Payment proof
* Document proof
* Donation proof
* Grant proof
* AI decision proof
* Service delivery proof

The first MVP will focus on simple proof creation and verification. Later versions will anchor proof records on Stellar/Soroban.
